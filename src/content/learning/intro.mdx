## Why this section exists

Learning is where I keep the map before the notes become polished articles. It is less about finished explanations and more about direction: what I am studying, why it matters, and how the pieces should connect.

The current focus is a practical stack:

- computer graphics foundations
- rendering math and shader intuition
- English and technical writing
- small interactive tools for checking understanding
- repeatable study habits

## How I want to use it

Each entry in this section should answer three questions:

1. What is the concept?
2. Where does it fit in the larger system?
3. What should I build, draw, or explain to know that I really understand it?

For now this page is the starting point. More learning notes can live beside it as separate MDX files under `src/content/learning`.

## Current direction

The first theme is turning abstract material into visible, testable ideas. If a topic can be simulated, plotted, animated, or reduced to a small experiment, it belongs here before it becomes a longer Journal or Works page.
